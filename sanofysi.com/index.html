    Prebiotic Syrup

    What is prebiotic syrup, and what is it used for?
    Sanofysi Prebiotic Syrup is a liquid fibre supplement made with 95% FOS, a soluble prebiotic fibre. It is used to add fibre to an everyday diet: you stir one spoon into tea, coffee, juice or yoghurt.
    How much fibre is in Sanofysi Prebiotic Syrup?
    One 15 mL spoon has 14.3 g of fibre, which is 52% of the daily value and about as much as 3½ bowls of oats. A 290 mL bottle holds about 19 servings.
    Is prebiotic syrup a healthy sugar alternative for tea and coffee?
    It is a lower-sugar way to sweeten a drink. It tastes mildly sweet, and one 15 mL spoon has 1.6 g of sugars and 35 calories, compared with about 12 g of sugars in a tablespoon of white sugar.
    What is the difference between a prebiotic and a probiotic?
    Probiotics are live bacteria. Prebiotics are the fibres those bacteria feed on. Prebiotic Syrup is a prebiotic.
    How do I take a fibre supplement like Prebiotic Syrup?
    Stir up to one 15 mL spoon a day into a drink or breakfast. If you are new to fibre, start with a teaspoon and build up over one to two weeks, and drink plenty of water.

    Sacre Crown hair fibres

    What are hair building fibres, and what are they made of?
    Hair building fibres are tiny coloured fibres you shake onto thinning areas, where they cling to your own hair and make it look fuller. Sacre Crown fibres are made of keratin fibres and colorant.
    Do hair fibres work on thinning hair, bald spots and hairlines?
    Sacre Crown Hair Building Fibers are made to cover thinning areas and bald patches in seconds and to create a fresh, natural-looking hairline. Fibres work best where there is some hair for them to hold on to.
    How do I use hair fibres on my hairline?
    Start with dry, styled hair. Shake the fibres over the thinning area and pat gently to blend. Use the applicator for a sharp hairline, then lock it in with Sacre Crown Hold Spray.
    Do hair fibres look natural, and are they for men and women?
    Yes. Sacre Crown fibres are made to look like real hair, and they are for both men and women. The current shade is black.
    Do hair fibres grow hair?
    No. Hair fibres are a cosmetic. They cover thinning areas instantly; they do not grow hair and are not a treatment for hair loss.
    Do hair fibres wash out?
    Yes. They stay in place until you wash your hair and come out with shampoo. The hold spray helps keep them in place through the day.
    Is hair fiber spray the same as hair fiber powder?
    Usually, yes. Hair fiber powder is the fibres shaken from the bottle. Hair fiber spray is the same fibres puffed through a spray applicator. Sacre Crown sells the fibres on their own and in a kit with the spray applicator and a hold spray.
    Is Sacre Crown hair fiber spray waterproof?
    The Sacre Crown Hold Spray is made to resist wind, rain and sweat, so the fibres stay in place through the day. They still wash out with shampoo.
    What is a thick fiber hair spray?
    It is another name for hair fibres used to make thin hair look thick. With Sacre Crown, the keratin fibres give the thickness and the Hold Spray gives the hold.
    Where can I buy hair fiber near me, and how much is it?
    Sacre Crown Hair Building Fibers and the Hair Perfecting Kit are $29 each at sacrecrown.sanofysi.com. Sanofysi delivers to every country in Africa, including Ghana, Nigeria and Kenya, and worldwide.
    Is Sacre Crown an alternative to Toppik, Sevich, Dexe or Bunee hair fiber?
    They are all hair fibre brands that work the same way. Sacre Crown is a separate brand made by Sanofysi, with black keratin fibres, a spray applicator and a hold spray, delivered across Africa. It is not connected to those brands.
