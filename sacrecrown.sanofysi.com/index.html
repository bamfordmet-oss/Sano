What are hair building fibers?

Hair building fibres are tiny coloured fibres that you shake onto thinning hair. They cling to the hair you already have, so each strand looks thicker and the scalp underneath shows less. Sacre Crown Hair Building Fibers are made from keratin fibres and colorant, and come in black.

Do hair fibers really work?

They work as a cover-up, and they work straight away. Fibres make thin areas look fuller for as long as you wear them. They need some hair to hold on to, so they suit thinning hair, sparse patches and soft hairlines better than a completely smooth bald area. They do not treat hair loss or make hair grow.

Do hair fibers look natural?

Yes, when the shade matches and you use a little at a time. Sacre Crown black fibres are made to blend into black hair. Shake lightly, pat them in, and step back from the mirror before adding more.

How do you use hair fibers on a hairline?

Start with dry, styled hair. Shake the fibres over the thin area and pat gently to blend. For the hairline itself, use the applicator from the Hair Perfecting Kit so the fibres land in a clean, sharp line. Finish with Hold Spray. See the full guide.

Are hair fibers good for black men and afro hair?

Yes. Sacre Crown was made with short, dark, textured hair in mind: fades, low cuts, waves and natural hair. The black shade is made to blend with black hair, and the applicator helps keep a barber-sharp hairline between cuts.

Can women use hair fibers?

Yes. Women use hair fibres on a widening parting, thin edges and sparse spots around the crown. The steps are the same: dry hair, a light shake, a gentle pat, then hold spray.

Do hair fibers wash out?

Yes. Hair fibres sit on the hair and come out when you shampoo. Put them on again after your hair is dry.

Are hair fibers waterproof? What about rain and sweat?

Fibres on their own can shift when they get wet. That is what the Sacre Crown Hold Spray is for: it locks the fibres in place and is made to resist wind, rain and sweat. They still wash out with shampoo.

Do hair fibers cause hair loss or damage the scalp?

Hair fibres are a cosmetic. They rest on the hair and wash out with shampoo, and they are not a treatment of any kind. Do not use them on broken or irritated skin, and stop if your scalp reacts. If you are worried about hair loss or a scalp condition, speak to a doctor or dermatologist.

What is in Sacre Crown Hair Building Fibers?

Two things, as listed on the label: keratin fibres and colorant. Each bottle holds 27.5 g (0.97 oz).

What is the difference between the fibres and the Hair Perfecting Kit?

The bottle is the hair fibres on their own. The Hair Perfecting Kit adds the two things that finish the job: the Hold Spray, which locks the fibres in place, and the spray applicator, which gives you a sharp hairline.

Is hair fiber spray the same as hair fibers?

Yes. A hair fiber spray is hair fibres put on through a spray applicator instead of shaken from the bottle. The fibres are the same. The Sacre Crown Hair Perfecting Kit includes the spray applicator.

What is the difference between hair spray and hair fiber spray?

Ordinary hair spray holds a style in place. Hair fiber spray adds fibres to your hair so it looks thicker. Sacre Crown Hold Spray is a hair spray made for one job: locking hair fibres in place.

Which fiber hair product gives thick-looking hair?

Look for three things: keratin fibres in a shade that matches your hair, an applicator for the hairline and a hold spray. The Hair Perfecting Kit has all three in one box.

Is Sacre Crown like Toppik, Sevich, Dexe or Bunee hair fiber?

They are all hair fibre brands built on the same idea: fibres that make thin hair look fuller.
